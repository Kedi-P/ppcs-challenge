id: PPCS-036
title: Add a context budget check to dispatch briefs
type: docs
difficulty: 1

## Brief
Teams are overloading dispatch briefs with too much history. Add a short
checklist to the participant materials that forces each brief to name the files,
tools, and traces the agent needs, plus what should stay out of context.

## Acceptance
- The checklist fits on one page.
- It asks what can be excluded from context.
- It reminds teams to clear/reset between unrelated tickets.
- It connects context size to reviewability and cost.

## Notes for the dispatcher
This is a low-code harness ticket. The strongest solution edits participant
material surgically rather than creating a sprawling new guide.
