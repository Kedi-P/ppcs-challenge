id: PPCS-052
title: Show margin risk beside each validation
type: feature
difficulty: 3

## Brief
Category managers want the workbench to show margin risk beside each SKU so they
can prioritise which failed validations to review first. Add a risk indicator
next to the validation result using the latest production margin data.

## Acceptance
- The workbench displays a margin-risk indicator beside each validation.
- The risk indicator is based on current SKU margin data.
- The implementation works in the workshop environment.
